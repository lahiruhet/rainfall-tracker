Rainfall is modelled. Open-Meteo provides ECMWF ERA5/IFS coordinate-based estimates, not rain-gauge measurements. The newest days may be preliminary.

River levels are measured gauge observations. Current readings come from the Sri Lanka Irrigation Department’s official ArcGIS dashboard. Earlier readings come from the community-maintained mirror. Values use the gauge datum, can be negative, and are never interpolated.

Thambuththegama uses the official coordinate in the Irrigation Department’s Hydrological Annual 2021/22. This public tracker is observational—not a forecast, flood-extent map, certification source, or nearest-gauge recommendation.
