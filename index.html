What this data actually is

Rainfall is modelled. Open-Meteo serves ECMWF ERA5 / IFS reanalysis: a model that blends multiple observations onto a grid. It is a useful coordinate-based estimate, not a rain-gauge measurement or certified station record.

River levels are measured gauge observations. Current readings come directly from the Sri Lanka Irrigation Department’s official public ArcGIS dashboard. The layer exposes only its latest seven days. Earlier readings from 28 November 2025 onward come from nuuuwan/lk_irrigation, a community-maintained snapshot mirror of that official feed—not an Irrigation Department archive.

Official flood-event spot check. The mirror’s Dunamale observation at 15:03 LKT on 28 November 2025 is 5.96 m, matching the approximately 15:00 reading in the official DMC water-level bulletin. This cross-check does not certify every archived observation.

The most recent rainfall days may be preliminary. River levels preserve gauge-datum values, including negatives, and missing dates are never interpolated. This is an observational tracker—not a flood forecast, discharge model, certification source, or automatic nearest-gauge recommendation.
